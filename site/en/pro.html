Built for book professionals

⚡ Fastest cataloging on the market

USB scanner + burst mode: ~2 seconds per book. French recognition (BnF, Google Books, Fnac) fills the records automatically — title, summary, cover, publisher.

📷 AI photo inventory

One shelf photo = a complete inventory with missing, misplaced and unknown items. Stocktaking takes minutes, not days.

📤 Compatible with Koha, PMB & UNIMARC

UNIMARC and MARC21 XML export: your records integrate into your existing ILS. And above all: holdings takeover — import your existing Koha/PMB catalog, every record becomes an app record, no duplicates. No lock-in: free export in JSON, CSV, MARC.

🏷️ QR labels & call numbers

Printable label generation (Dewey call numbers + item QR codes): scan QR = record opens, lending and returning in one gesture.

📤 Loans & members

Borrowers, due dates, late flags, complete per-person history, reservations, reminders on launch.
