Everything your library needs

📷 Barcode scanning

Camera (phone) or USB scanner (PC). Burst mode, automatic shelving by bookcase and shelf, duplicate detection.

🤖 AI shelf photo

One photo, the AI reads the spines row by row and compares with your collection: missing, misplaced, unknown.

🇫🇷 French recognition

4 sources including the BnF national library — unmatched for the French backlist, including pre-2010 editions.

📤 Loans & inventory

Borrowers, due dates, history, reservations. Shelf inventory with a missing/intruder report.

📖 Reading tracking

Annual goal, "read next" queue, page progress, dated journal, complete statistics.

💾 Multiple exports

JSON, CSV, HTML, Markdown, spine labels and QR codes — and UNIMARC/MARC21 (pro ILS). Merging CSV/JSON import.

☁️ Private cloud backup

Your books in your private GitHub repository, with version history. Multi-device: push from the PC, pull on mobile.

Your data stays with you

No account. No server. Your whole library lives in your browser — exportable anytime, never held hostage.
